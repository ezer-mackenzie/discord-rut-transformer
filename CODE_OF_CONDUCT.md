# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Accept responsibility and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information, including real RUTs.

Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing
another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer at
ramirez.ruiz.eliezer.reuven@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, temporarily ban,
or permanently ban participants according to the severity and recurrence of the
behavior.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
